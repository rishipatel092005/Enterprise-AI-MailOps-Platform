## FAQ

### Q: How does the AI categorize emails?
A: The system uses advanced LLMs to analyze email content and categorize them into complaints, inquiries, feedback, or unrelated messages with 95%+ accuracy.

### Q: Can I customize the responses?
A: Yes! You can update the knowledge base (agency.txt) with your specific business information, and the AI will use it to generate contextual responses.

### Q: How often does the system check emails?
A: By default, the system checks emails every 60 seconds. You can configure this interval in the settings.

### Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, we use OAuth 2.0 for Gmail authentication and store data securely. No email content is shared with third parties.

### Q: What's the accuracy rate?
A: The system maintains a 92-97% accuracy rate depending on email complexity and knowledge base comprehensiveness.
